Update Delays & Long Periods of Absence
Devlog — 09/09/2026

Summer Status

Upon the start of summer break in my country, I had updates planned to make a release, but I haven't had time to focus on it, and so I was never active on the mod for 3 months: June, July & most of August.

I had a plan to make a new mini mod that is independent from Create: Beyond Limits, and yet I stopped almost at the finish line after crashing out on the entity's behavior.

Teased on my X (left) and featured at the top of the new Create: Beyond Limits icon (right)

Upcoming Updates (2.0.0 & 2.5.0)

It's September at the time I am writing this, and I have also revealed the next update (2.0.0) with its logical sequel: Thermal Heating & Thermal Cooling (the 2.0.0 & 2.5.0 updates).

As for what I was planning, I planned to release the update to the public (2.0.0) at the end of the month. But as always, code block hits, code stops functioning, I stop thinking straight and start making garbage.

As of 09/09/26, the main structure of the update has been made—from textures, models, and recipes to fluid transfer and the Conductor, which is the main block of the update. Yet systems are not fully functional: branching isn't working as intended, conductors bug out and crash the game in specific scenarios. I don't want to rush the update and yet I don't want to take a lot of time on it. In addition, school is starting 09/15/26, which is going to slow down the process even more.

I will and always apologize for my absence and the update delays, although it's not under my hands to handle, as I am a human being that stresses, gets sick, has a family to spend time with, and a teenager that gets mood swings and headaches all the time.

I thank you all for your support and affection by downloading my mod.
