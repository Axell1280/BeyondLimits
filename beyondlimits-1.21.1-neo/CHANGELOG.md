# Create Beyond Limits — v1.0.0

## Full Release
This update marks the full release of the mod, transitioning to the NeoForge 1.21.1 platform. Please note that the Forge 1.20.1 version is now deprecated.

## System Overhauls

### Advancement Overhaul
Advancement triggers reworked. Progression paths cleaned up to ensure goals are easier to track and verify during gameplay.

### Recipe and Loot Balance
Recipes and loot tables updated across the mod. Progression flow adjusted for a more natural experience, with rebalanced rewards for exploration and crafting.

## Future Plans

### Mechanical Sprinklers
Design phase initiated for a new mechanical sprinkler system. Current plans involve automated crop hydration utilizing mechanical power and fluid piping. This feature remains in early conceptual stages.
