Update 2.0.0 — Thermal Heating
Devlog — Release Notes

Update Overview

Thermal Heating is officially here! This major update introduces a brand-new mechanical heat distribution system, 4 new functional blocks, complex thermal mechanics to automate, and 8 unique advancements to complete.

Features & Advancements

The core mechanism revolves around managing fluid temperature, heat line loss, and routing thermal energy across your factory setups.

New Thermal Blocks
  • Thermal Siphon Drain
  • Thermal Pipes
  • Thermal Conductor
  • Thermal Outflow
New System Advancements
  • "Heat, Meet Machine"
  • "Closing the Loop"
  • "Branch Manager"
  • "All Back to One"
  • "Line Loss"
  • "Wide Open Throttle"
  • "Borrowed Warmth"
  • "Scorched Earth"

Beta Notes & Future Teaser

This version is currently in Beta. Expect minor bugs! The Thermal Conductor currently has a known visual/model issue that will be resolved in upcoming hotfix patch updates.
